# Cetirizine 10 mg tablets

Cetirizine is a listed over-the-counter second-generation antihistamine for hay fever, allergic rhinitis and mild allergic skin itching in adults. Usual adult dose is 10 mg once daily. It may cause drowsiness in some people, so caution is advised when driving. See [[POLICY-ESCALATION]], [[SAFETY-PEDIATRIC]].
