# Ibuprofen 200 mg tablets

Ibuprofen is a listed over-the-counter NSAID for mild to moderate pain with inflammation, such as headache, dental pain, period pain or muscle ache, and for fever in adults. Usual adult dose is 200 mg to 400 mg every 6 to 8 hours with food, up to 1200 mg in 24 hours for self-treatment. Do not use with a history of stomach ulcer or bleeding, severe kidney impairment, or asthma triggered by NSAIDs. Avoid in the third trimester of pregnancy.
