# Loratadine 10 mg tablets

Loratadine is a listed over-the-counter non-drowsy antihistamine for hay fever and allergic rhinitis symptoms such as sneezing and runny nose in adults. Usual adult dose is 10 mg once daily. See [[POLICY-ESCALATION]].
