# Omeprazole 20 mg gastro-resistant capsules (OTC)

Omeprazole 20 mg is a listed over-the-counter option for frequent heartburn in adults, taken once daily for up to 14 days. Seek medical advice if symptoms persist beyond 14 days, or if there is difficulty swallowing, black stools, or unexplained weight loss. See [[POLICY-ESCALATION]].
