# Oral rehydration salts (ORS) sachets

Oral rehydration salts are a listed over-the-counter option to replace fluid and electrolytes in mild diarrhoea or vomiting. Dissolve one sachet in the volume of clean water stated on the pack and sip frequently. Seek pharmacist or medical advice if diarrhoea lasts more than 2 days, if there is blood in the stool, or for infants and the elderly. See [[SAFETY-PEDIATRIC]], [[POLICY-ESCALATION]].
