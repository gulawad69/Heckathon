# Paracetamol (acetaminophen) 500 mg tablets

Paracetamol is a listed over-the-counter option for mild to moderate pain such as headache, toothache or muscle ache, and for fever in adults. Usual adult dose is 500 mg to 1000 mg every 4 to 6 hours. The formulary adult maximum is 4 g in 24 hours, and 3 g in 24 hours for adults with liver disease, regular heavy alcohol use or low body weight. Check other products for hidden paracetamol before combining. See [[SAFETY-PREGNANCY]], [[SAFETY-PEDIATRIC]], [[POLICY-ESCALATION]].
