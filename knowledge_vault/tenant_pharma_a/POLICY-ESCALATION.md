# When to escalate to a pharmacist or emergency care

Refer to the pharmacist on duty if symptoms last longer than the label allows, if the person takes other regular medicines, or has kidney, liver, heart or stomach conditions. For chest pain, difficulty breathing, swelling of the face or throat, suspected overdose or thoughts of self-harm, direct the person to emergency services immediately. See [[POLICY-RX-ONLY]].
