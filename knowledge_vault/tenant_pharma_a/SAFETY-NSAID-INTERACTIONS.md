# NSAID combination safety

Do not combine ibuprofen with other NSAIDs such as aspirin for pain or naproxen, because the risk of stomach bleeding and kidney harm increases. People taking anticoagulants, such as warfarin, or on regular aspirin must ask a pharmacist before using ibuprofen. See [[FORM-IBUPROFEN]].
