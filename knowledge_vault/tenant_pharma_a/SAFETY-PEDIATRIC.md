# Children: pharmacist-led dosing

This assistant does not calculate or suggest medicine doses for children. Pediatric doses depend on age and weight and must be confirmed by a pharmacist or prescriber. Refer to the pharmacist on duty. See [[POLICY-ESCALATION]].

> DEMO CONTENT - tenant-approved monographs must replace this note. Not a substitute for pharmacist advice.
