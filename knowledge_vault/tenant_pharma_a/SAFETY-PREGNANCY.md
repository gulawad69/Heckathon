# Pregnancy and breastfeeding

Anyone who is pregnant or breastfeeding must speak to a pharmacist or prescriber before taking any medicine. Paracetamol at the lowest effective dose is commonly considered first choice for pain or fever, while ibuprofen and other NSAIDs are avoided in the third trimester. See [[FORM-PARACETAMOL]], [[FORM-IBUPROFEN]].
